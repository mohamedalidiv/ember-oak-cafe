Our Heritage

Crafting Moments in Every Cup

Ember & Oak was born from a passion for the tactile and the timeless. Our roastery focuses on ethical sourcing and meticulous roasting profiles that highlight the unique terroir of every harvest.

Step into a space where the warmth of reclaimed oak meets the precision of modern brewing. We believe that a great cup of coffee is the foundation of community and quiet reflection.
